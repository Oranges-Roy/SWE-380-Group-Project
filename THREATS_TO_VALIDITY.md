### Dataset Noise & Template Artifacts (Construct Validity)
* **Threat:** Mined software repositories often contain boilerplate, placeholder files, or empty `SKILL.md` templates (`content_fetched = 0`). Because empty or minimal files share identical structural formats, unmitigated similarity algorithms can risk assigning false-positive high similarity scores (approaching 1.0) to trivial placeholders.
* **Mitigation & Resolution:** To counteract this, our Sprint 2 pipeline explicitly audits and filters content during extraction (`has_content` validation). Unfetched records and empty templates are excluded prior to vectorization to prevent artificial inflation of unique metrics. Furthermore, our pipeline accounts for Git symlink stubs (`looks_like_symlink`) to ensure path-only blobs are correctly categorized without acting as false semantic clones.
### External Validity & Dataset Generalizability
* **Threat:** Our analysis is restricted to Python, C++, and other selected languages within the mined dataset, which may not fully represent coding and documentation conventions in niche or private repositories.
* **Mitigation:** While specific token frequencies may vary by language ecosystem, our modular pipeline design (`run_language_pipeline.py`) ensures that these clone detection and threshold heuristics can be scaled consistently across any repository.
